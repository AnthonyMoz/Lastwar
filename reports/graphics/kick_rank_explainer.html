How the kick score works

Every member gets a score from 0 to 100 based on the last 4 full weeks. Higher means more at risk of being kicked. 0 is perfect.

55MAX PTS

VS days under the minimum

The share of VS days you scored under 7.2M. Under on every day = 55 points. Under on half the days = 27.5.

35MAX PTS

Donation shortfall

How far your weekly average falls short of 35,000 (5,000 a day). Donate nothing = 35 points. Hit the target = 0.

10PTS

Power going down

If your power is lower at the end of the 4 weeks than at the start, you get 10 points. Growing or flat = 0.

Rank sorts everyone by score. Rank 1 is the highest score, the member most at risk. Officers review the top of the list. Nobody is kicked on the number alone.

Not ranked

  • New members: joined in the last 14 days
  • Away or rejoined: no VS record on over a quarter of days. Officers check in game.
  • Excused by officers, e.g. holidays
  • Storms don't count. Officers pick the teams.

Stay off the list

  • Score 7.2M+ VS every VS day
  • Donate 5,000 a day (35,000 a week)
  • Keep your power growing
  • Away for a while? Tell an R4
